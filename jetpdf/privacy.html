Jet PDF – Privacy Policy

NozikStudio · Effective date: 19 November 2026

This policy explains how the Android app Jet PDF ("the app"), made by NozikStudio, handles information. In short: your documents stay on your phone. We do not run any servers that receive your files.

1. Your files and documents

All PDF viewing, editing, signing, form filling, merging, compressing, converting and text recognition (OCR) happen on your device. The app does not upload, store or share your documents, photos or signatures anywhere. New files you create are saved only to your phone (for example in Downloads/Jet PDF or Pictures/Jet PDF). Your drawn signature is stored only inside the app's private storage on your phone.

2. Permissions we use

  • All files access / storage – only to find and list the PDF files on your phone so you can open them. File contents are never sent off the device.
  • Camera (document scanner) – the scanner is provided by Google Play services (ML Kit Document Scanner). Images are processed on your device and returned only to the app.
  • Internet – to show ads and to download optional text-recognition language packs.

3. Text recognition language packs

For Georgian and Russian text recognition the app downloads a language pack once from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com, the public Tesseract OCR project). Only a normal file download request is made; no personal data or documents are sent. English recognition uses Google ML Kit, which runs on your device via Google Play services.

4. Advertising

The app is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device model and approximate location to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown; you can change your choice at any time in the app menu under Privacy settings. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

5. Purchases

If in-app purchases are offered, they are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

6. Data we store on your device

The app keeps small settings on your phone: recent files, favorites, bookmarks, reading position, chosen language and display settings. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.

7. Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell personal data. Apart from the advertising data handled by Google AdMob described above, the app does not share data with third parties.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
